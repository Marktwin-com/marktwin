# Code of Conduct

Marktwin contributors are expected to keep discussion respectful, technical, and
focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction when impact differs from intent.
- Keep criticism tied to code, documentation, design, or project decisions.
- Respect privacy and security reports. Do not pressure reporters to disclose
  sensitive details publicly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Dismissing security or privacy concerns without investigation.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests after maintainers ask you to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests;
or block contributors whose behavior harms the project or community. To report a
conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately using the security contact in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
